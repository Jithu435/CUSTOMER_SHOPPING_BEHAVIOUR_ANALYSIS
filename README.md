# CUSTOMER_SHOPPING_BEHAVIOUR_ANALYSIS
End-to-end data analysis project exploring customer shopping behaviors, purchasing patterns. Analyzes customer segmentation, product performance, and sales drivers to optimize business strategy using Python, Pandas, and SQL.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end analysis of customer shopping behavior, purchasing patterns, and sales drivers. By analyzing customer transaction history, the goal is to provide actionable business insights that help optimize marketing strategies, inventory management, and customer retention.

## 🔑 Key Objectives
* **Demographic Segmentation:** Analyze spending patterns across age groups, genders, and locations.
* **Product Performance:** Identify top-performing product categories, seasonal buying trends, and revenue drivers.
* **Purchase Channels & Behavior:** Evaluate preferred payment methods, subscription adoption, and shopping frequencies.
* **Strategic Recommendations:** Pinpoint opportunities for targeted promotions and repeat-purchase drivers.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Querying & Storage:** SQL
* **Data Processing & Manipulation:** Python, Pandas
* **Environment:** Google Colab
*  **Visualization:** PowerBI
